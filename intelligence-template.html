        This monitor checks completed longevity trial registrations. A possible gap means the indexed registry record shows no posted results more than 12 months after its listed completion date.         How to interpret a flag

        A registry can lack structured results even when findings appear in a journal, conference, sponsor report, another registry, or a delayed update. Some studies are not subject to the same reporting rules. Dates and statuses can change. Open the original registry record before drawing a conclusion.

          What this ranking does—and does not—mean

          The index measures publication activity within the configured longevity topics. It does not rank teaching, clinical care, study quality, safety, effectiveness, or the value of an institution. OpenAlex affiliation matching can miss or misassign organizations, and highly collaborative works can be credited to several universities. Select any university to inspect its topic and source details.

          How sources are included

          We list official authorities, WHO-recognised trial registries, and established public research infrastructure. “Live data” means records are indexed here; “Official link” opens the source directly. Links are checked automatically. A listing is not an endorsement, and registry inclusion does not prove that a treatment works. Regulatory information applies only to the country or region named by the authority.
