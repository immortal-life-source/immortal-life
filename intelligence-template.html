    What this ranking does—and does not—mean

    The index measures publication activity within the configured longevity topics. It does not rank teaching, clinical care, study quality, safety, effectiveness, or the value of an institution. OpenAlex affiliation matching can miss or misassign organizations, and highly collaborative works can be credited to several universities.     Explore the evidence directory

    Start with what you need. Find a registered trial, verify a medicine with the relevant national authority, read a systematic review, or explore datasets built for ageing research. Every link opens the original source.

    How sources are included

    We list official authorities, WHO-recognised trial registries, and established public research infrastructure. Links are checked automatically every six hours. A listing is not an endorsement. Registry inclusion does not prove that a treatment works. Regulatory information applies only to the country or region, product, and use named by the original authority.
