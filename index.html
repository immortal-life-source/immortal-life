Manifesto
For 200,000 years, death was not a problem to be solved. It was simply the condition of being alive. That assumption is being revised — in laboratories, in clinical trials, in the data coming back from the first generation of interventions that treat ageing as a biological process rather than an inevitability.
The science has crossed a threshold. The question now is whether you are paying attention. Billions in research capital. Thousands of scientists. Convergent technologies arriving simultaneously — AI-accelerated drug discovery, epigenetic reprogramming, cellular rejuvenation. The timeline is compressing faster than public awareness.

Privacy Policy

immortal.life · Last updated: September 2026
Who we are

immortal.life is a fully automated publication focused on longevity science and research. It has no scientists, clinicians, researchers, editors, or human reviewers. We are the data controller for personal information you provide through this website.

What we collect

For newsletter subscribers, we collect your email address, consent record, submission time, and source page. If you join the members area, we also process the limited profile details returned by your selected sign-in provider (X or LinkedIn), the provider-specific member identifier, invite activity, points, watchlists, briefings, and account timestamps. LinkedIn sign-in is used for account access and is not identity verification. We do not request medical or health information.

Why we collect it

Your email address is used for communications you requested. Social profile data is used to create and secure your member account, operate invitations and rankings, and provide personalised watchlists and briefings. OAuth access tokens are used transiently during sign-in and are not stored by immortal.life.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Newsletter data is processed on the basis of consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Member-account data is processed as necessary to provide the service you request (Article 6(1)(b)) and, where applicable, for proportionate security and abuse-prevention interests (Article 6(1)(f)).

Where your data is stored

We use contracted infrastructure providers to host the website, database, authentication, and email delivery. Where processing involves an international transfer, the operator uses the transfer safeguards required by applicable data-protection law. Provider and transfer details will be maintained in the full privacy notice.

How long we keep it

We retain your email address while you remain subscribed and member data while your account is active. You can delete the member account from the dashboard. If you unsubscribe or request deletion, applicable personal data is removed within 30 days unless law requires longer retention.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to: access the data we hold about you; request correction or deletion; withdraw consent at any time; lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@immortal.life.

Cookies

This page uses cookieless traffic analytics and bounded daily counts of useful actions such as opening a source or downloading a dataset. The product counters contain no visitor identifier, IP address, browser fingerprint, cookie, or free-form text.
